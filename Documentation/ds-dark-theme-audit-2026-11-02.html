Root cause

Steps 8–10 of the palettes hold dark-theme colours

The Colors collection has a single mode. In 11 Ant palettes, steps 8, 9 and 10 were replaced by Ant's dark steps 3, 2 and 1. Dark mode only looks right for Bg and Border because of this, and Light tokens that point at steps 8–9 get near-black colours.

Red outline = step that doesn't match Ant's light paletteAffected: Red, Volcano, Orange, Gold, Yellow, Lime, Cyan, Blue, Geek Blue, Purple, Magenta. Green-8 and Hydra-8 kept their light value.

What already works

Neutral text ladder in Dark
  • Neutral fills and borders match Ant dark exactly: white 18 / 12 / 8 / 4% fills, 20 / 12% borders, Spotlight #434343.
  • Text colours swap to lighter tints in Dark (e.g. Info text → blue-4). Not Ant-exact, but Ant's dark text colours would only reach about 2–3.5:1 on navy.
  • Presets Bg, BgHover, Border and Text are consistent across all 12 colours. Only Magenta BorderHover is off.
  • Surface order is right: layout #0A2238 < container #0C3559 < elevated #004173.
  • Sidebar, shadows, mask, white are the same in both modes by design.
  • All 56 numeric tokens (size, padding, margin, radius, line width, breakpoints) are identical in both modes, as they should be.

Risk · 33 links

Dark relies on steps 8–10 whose names don't match their colour

These Dark links look right only because steps 8–10 hold Ant's dark colours. If someone corrects the palettes to the real light values, all 33 break. Fix the palette and relink in the same change. Two are already wrong: green-8 and hydra-8 were never swapped.

Collision

Dark surfaces share the brand navy primitives

Container primary-8, elevated primary-7 and layout primary-10 also drive colorSidebar and colorPrimaryActive. colorPrimaryBorderSubtle is primary-8 too, so on the container it disappears.

If the brand blue changes, the whole Dark background changes with it. Dedicated dark-surface primitives would separate them.

Smaller points

  • Status backgrounds start from different steps. colorErrorBg is built from red-5 while colorError uses red-6. colorInfoBg is built from blue-6.
  • Dark text greys mix solid and see-through. Most link to solid gray-3/5/6/7, but colorTextDisabled is gray-1 @35%. Light uses black alpha for all of them.
  • colorBgSpotlight in Light uses opacity-100 @85% instead of a primitive.
Decision needed

Figma Dark is navy, the code's Dark is neutral grey

The right fix for borders, main colours and backgrounds depends on which base is correct. On navy: alpha backgrounds for every status, or Ant ramps regenerated against navy. On neutral: Ant's standard dark ramps, plus moving Figma surfaces to grey.
